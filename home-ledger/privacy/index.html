Home Ledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Home Ledger stores only what you put into it to plan, schedule and budget your home projects, and never sells it. This policy covers the Home Ledger iPhone app: what it collects, where that data lives, who else touches it, and how to delete it. "We" means the independent developer who makes Home Ledger.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive what Apple shares, which may be a private relay address and your name.
  • What you enter: tasks, dates, notes, costs, shopping lists, your hourly value, home fund and budget figures, and your home's nickname.
  • Photos and details you add: for example appliance labels, model and serial numbers, paint codes and warranty dates. Photos are resized on your phone before upload.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health data or advertising identifiers, and the app has no analytics or ad trackers.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in the Americas region, and travels only over encrypted connections. Database rules let each account read and change only its own records. Photos sit in private storage and are shown through links that expire after one hour. The app also keeps a copy on your phone so you can view your plans offline. All budget and DIY-versus-hire calculations run on your phone.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade your data.
  • We don't show ads or track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't share your data with data brokers or marketers.
  • We don't send your data to AI services. Anything shared with an AI app happens only when you choose to share it (next section).

Because the app doesn't track you across other sites or apps, it doesn't respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

What leaves the app only when you tap

  • Share with AI: opens your iPhone's share menu with the photo and a short message. The app you pick, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Shopping buttons: open a retailer's website with a product search. No personal details are included.
  • Calendar events, reminders and exports: stay on your phone unless you send an export somewhere yourself.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete anything you've entered, at any time, in the app.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, tasks and photos. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short period before they are overwritten.
  • Take your plans with you: export your schedule to Excel or CSV from the app.
  • Questions or requests: contact us (below) and we'll respond within 30 days.

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, or until you delete it.

Children, changes and contact

Home Ledger is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app. Questions: contact us at info@buildcurrent.com.
